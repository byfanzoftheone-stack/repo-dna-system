## 💡 Key Concepts

### Technical Debt
Estimated effort (in days) needed to fix quality issues and technical debt.
- **Low**: <7 days
- **Medium**: 7-30 days
- **High**: >30 days

### Complexity Score
Cyclomatic complexity average for functions.
- **Low**: <3 (easy to understand)
- **Medium**: 3-5 (manageable)
- **High**: 5-10 (complex)
- **Critical**: >10 (needs refactoring)

### Reusability Score
1-10 rating for component reusability.
- **9-10**: Extract & publish as library
- **7-8**: Share internally
- **5-6**: Consider extracting
- **<5**: Not reusable as-is

### Architectural Patterns
- **Monolith**: All code in single codebase
- **Layered/MVC**: Clear tier separation
- **Microservices**: Independent services
- **Event-Driven**: Async messaging
- **Hexagonal**: Domain-centric design
