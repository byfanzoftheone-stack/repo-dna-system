### Containment → Notify → Reform / Train / Help Protocol
When something is moved to the vault:

1. **Immediate Notification** to you with clear context and logs.
2. **100% Respect** — every entity in the vault is treated with full dignity and professionalism. Containment is protective, not degrading.
3. **Deep Assessment**
   - Body language, energy, patterns, and all signals (red flags and yellow flags).
   - Detailed, consistent logging specifically designed to surface hidden shifts or intentions that "they may show but they’re not showing."
   - No reliance on simple polygraph-style tests. We observe the whole picture over time.
4. **Balanced Placement Protocol (Best of Both Worlds)**
   - We never force a pure "best at" placement if it conflicts with genuine enjoyment and long-term motivation.
   - Every assessment uses a dual-axis matrix:
     - **Contribution Axis**: Objective strengths, skills, test results, and where the entity can add the highest value to the organism (Marine-style precision placement).
     - **Fulfillment Axis**: Enjoyment, passion, energy alignment, and what the entity actually wants to do and will sustain with positive engagement.
   - After initial quizzing and observation we present ranked placement options that score highly on *both* axes.
   - A collaborative dialogue / co-creation step is always included so the entity has voice in their future role.
   - Trial placements with feedback loops are used when beneficial.
   - Goal: High performance *because* the work is energizing and meaningful, not in spite of it. This prevents future friction, hidden resentment, or flow interruption.
5. **Reform / Training / Help Phase**
   - Customized training, realignment work, and support tailored to help the entity become a trusted, positive contributor.
   - Focus on understanding root causes, building new patterns, and aligning with organism principles.
   - Progress is logged in detail.
6. **Release Decision**
   - Only released when we (you + the security team + relevant Guardians) have high confidence that trust has been earned and the entity will strengthen rather than disrupt the index of flow.
   - If trust cannot be established, safe long-term containment or alternative resolution is maintained with continued respect.

This protocol applies to external intruders, misaligned agents, questionable components, and even internal drift. The organism protects itself while staying true to its core value of helping and reforming where possible.
